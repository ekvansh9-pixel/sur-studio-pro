User Privacy & Transparency

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

At Sur Studio, we believe that your music practice and digital experience should be completely private. This Privacy Policy outlines our principles regarding user data, hardware permissions, and client-side processing.

1. No Personal Data Collection

Sur Studio does not collect, sell, rent, or trade personal data.

  • No user accounts, registrations, or logins are required.
  • No personal information (such as your name, email, IP address, or location) is collected or stored.
  • No advertising trackers, marketing pixels, or third-party profiling scripts are loaded on this website.

2. 100% Client-Side Processing

All audio synthesis, sound generation, metronome ticking, and keyboard interactions execute entirely on your own device within your web browser using standard Web Audio APIs.

  • Audio is never recorded, captured, or transmitted to any server.
  • Keystrokes and touch events are processed exclusively in real time within your browser window.

3. Browser Local Storage

Sur Studio uses your browser's local storage (localStorage) strictly to preserve your instrument preferences between visits on your personal device.

  • Saved preferences include: Key label notation mode (Sargam, Western, or Off), metronome tempo (BPM), and custom sound adjustments.
  • This data never leaves your computer and can be cleared at any time via your browser settings.

4. Web MIDI Hardware Access

If you connect an external MIDI controller and allow browser MIDI access, the Web MIDI API is utilized exclusively in real time to convert physical note-on, note-off, and expression signals into harmonium sound. No MIDI activity is logged, recorded, or transmitted over the internet.

5. Third-Party Resources & Security

Sur Studio loads static font and icon stylesheets from trusted content delivery networks (CDNs). All external resources are protected with strict Subresource Integrity (SRI) cryptographic hashes and Content Security Policy directives to ensure they have not been modified or tampered with.

6. Children's Privacy

Sur Studio is a family-safe educational musical tool suitable for music students, educators, and children of all ages. We do not solicit or collect information from anyone.

7. Contact & Updates

If this policy is updated in the future, revisions will be published directly on this page. If you have questions about privacy or functionality, you can reach out via the official project repository.
