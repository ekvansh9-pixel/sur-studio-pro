About the Instrument

Overview

Sur Studio is a modern, high-fidelity browser-based digital harmonium and precision metronome designed for musicians, students, vocalists, and educators. It provides an authentic acoustic harmonium experience right inside your web browser without requiring any software installations, plugins, or downloads.

Built on standard Web Audio and Web MIDI APIs, Sur Studio combines responsive multi-reed resonance with deep tone-shaping controls, flexible musical notation systems, and hardware instrument connectivity.

Core Features

  • Realistic 23-Key Keyboard: Full range covering Mandra, Madhya, and Taar Saptak (C3 to A#4) with ivory and ebony keys, touch glissando, and visual key-press illumination.
  • Dual Sound Architecture: Features high-fidelity multi-reed acoustic sample playback paired with an automatic procedural additive synthesis fallback for offline or restricted environments.
  • Precision Web Audio Metronome: A sample-accurate, drift-free metronome running on the hardware audio clock (30–240 BPM), featuring Tap Tempo, customizable time signatures (4/4, 3/4, 6/8), independent volume, and visual downbeat pulse indicator.
  • Musical Notation Flexibility: Instant toggling between Bhatkhande Indian Sargam notation (Sa, Re, Ga, Ma, Pa, Dha, Ni with register indicators), Western note names, or unlabeled keys.
  • Concert Pitch & Transposition: Semitone transposition from -11 to +11 semitones with live tonic root tracking (C through B) and register octave shifting.
  • Tone Shaping & Acoustic Environment: Dynamic additional reed layers (0–4) with micro-detuning, envelope controls (attack and release), a 3-band equalizer (Bass, Mid, Treble), acoustic convolution reverb, and an integrated dynamics limiter to prevent clipping.
  • Hardware Web MIDI: Connect any USB or Bluetooth MIDI controller keyboard for zero-latency physical playing, channel omni-monitoring, and hardware CC7 volume pedal integration.
  • Custom Audio Sampler: Load custom external sample URLs or local audio files from your device to tailor the timbre to your specific requirements.

Designed For

  • Classical Vocal Riyaz: Perfect for daily vocal warmups, alankars, swara practice, and raga exploration with accurate reference pitches.
  • Music Education & Theory: Visualizing notes, sargam scales, intervals, and rhythm structures clearly on desktop or classroom displays.
  • Accompaniment & Songwriting: A reliable, lightweight companion for practicing melodies and checking chords wherever you are.

Universal Device Support

Sur Studio is fully responsive and optimized for desktop computers, laptops, tablets, and smartphones running modern web browsers including Google Chrome, Apple Safari, Microsoft Edge, and Mozilla Firefox.
